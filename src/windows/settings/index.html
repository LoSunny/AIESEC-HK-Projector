How shortcuts work

  • Shortcuts control the source selected with Present tab, even when you are previewing a different tab.
  • Your clicker works while another app has focus. Keep the clicker connected when presenting.
  • Connect your clicker, choose Change, and press its matching slide button. Choose Save shortcut to finish, or Clear to turn that action off.
  • Shortcuts apply immediately after saving and need to be set again after restarting the app.
